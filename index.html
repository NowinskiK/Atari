About

Disks

A game runs from a disk image in drive D1: (an executable, .xex, is put on a write-protected boot disk). What you save goes to that image in the browser and is lost when you close or reload the page, so use Download disk to keep it and Load disk… to put it back later. Loading or creating a disk swaps it in D1: like changing a floppy; Restart boots from it. A loaded .xex boots at once.
