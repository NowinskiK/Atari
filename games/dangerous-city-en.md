*Dangerous City* is a top-down adventure game written by Kamil Nowiński in 1994, when he was 16. He prepared two
versions, one with the Mirage logo and one with the LK Avalon logo, but neither company released it. The game was
recovered from his old floppy disks, and in 2023 the Polish Society for the Protection of Technical Heritage (PTODT)
published it as a boxed cartridge with a manual and a map. This file is the English version of that 2023 edition
(version 2.129).

On your way home from filming your new movie, you take a wrong exit and end up in a mysterious city with no people and
no way out. Drive through its streets, pick up the items you find, work out what each one is for and use it in the
right place: that is how you open the gate that leads out of the city. There is no time limit, and the map has 71
screens. Watch the gauges at the sides of the screen: the game ends when you run out of fuel (fill up at the petrol
station) or when the car takes too much damage from hitting things off the road.

Steer left or right to choose a direction, then hold fire to drive: the car moves until you release fire or hit an
obstacle. You collect an item by driving over it. You can carry four items and cannot put them down; an item leaves the
list only when you use it.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| START (here: F2) or fire | start the game |
| HELP (here: F1) | the legend on the title screen |
| 1 – 4 | use the item in that slot |
| M | music on / off |
| Esc | end the game |

The 2023 edition added colour to the game screens, a new title screen, numbered item slots, fuel and damage icons,
secret codes and half the fuel consumption, and fixed a few bugs. The English version is new in it. The full list is in
the [change log](https://github.com/NowinskiK/DangerousCity/blob/main/ChangeLog.md).

Original game: code **Kamil Nowiński**, music **Konrad Gmurek**, title graphics **Hubert Nowiński**, screenplay and map
**Kamil & Hubert Nowiński**. Edition 2023: title graphics **Krzysztof "Kaz" Ziembik**, technical support **PEBE & Mono**,
cartridge **Jakub Husak**, box design **Rafał Dudek, Piotr "Piter" Krużycki, Krzysztof "Kaz" Ziembik**.
