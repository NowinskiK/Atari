*Fred* is a scrolling platform game about a caveman, written by Mirosław Liminowicz and Janusz Pelc; Pelc also
made the graphics and the music. It was made at the Polish company LK Avalon, which sold it in Poland on disk and
cassette (together with Pelc's *Misja*), and in late 1991 licensed it with *Misja* to Zeppelin Games for Western
Europe – this file is the Zeppelin version (© 1991 Zeppelin Games). KE-Soft (Germany) and ANG Software (the
Netherlands) published it too. Sources give 1990 or 1991 for the Polish release.

As the title screen puts it, you have to survive in a land full of adventures and dangers: travel through the wild
land and skilfully use the things you find on your way. Before you start you choose your weapon – **stones** or a
**spray** (the game says: stones right and down, spray left and down) – and you can select the level. Fred is
played with the joystick (here: arrows, and Space or Ctrl to fire).

In 2014 Heptomino and LK Avalon announced ports of *Fred* and *Lasermania* to iOS and Android, keeping the
original graphics.
