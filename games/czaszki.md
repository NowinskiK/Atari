*Czaszki* ("Skulls") is a logic game made by Tecumseh Software for LK Avalon, which published it in 1993. The title
screen credits Paweł Grotowski with the program, graphics and music, and Paweł and Marek Grotowski with the design of
the rooms. Atarimania also names Irek and K. Szczesniak among the authors.

You guide a little man from room to room. The souls of the Indians are waiting to find peace, and when they do, the
world is saved from their revenge.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| START (here: F2) or fire | start the game |
| joystick or keyboard (here: arrows) | move the little man |
| OPTION (here: F4) | music or sound effects |
| Esc | self-destruct |
| Space | pause |

All the texts are in Polish.
