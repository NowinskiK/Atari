*Robbo NK* is a home-made *Robbo*: 34 new planets built with *Robbo Konstruktor* by Kamil, Hubert
and Henryk Nowiński, and saved as a stand-alone game. It introduces itself as "the next of very many versions of Robbo"
and was never a commercial release. This is part 1. Its ending promises *Robbo NK* part 2 for mid-1993, so it was made
before then. The game also says that a *Robbo NK demo* came first, and that if you finished the demo you may manage this
one too.

It plays like the original: collect the screws on each planet, then reach the capsule that flies you to the next one.
Play with the joystick (here: arrows, and Space or Ctrl to fire). OPTION (here: F4) on the title screen runs the
demonstration, and fire stops it. All the texts are in Polish.

Sources: the game's own texts.
