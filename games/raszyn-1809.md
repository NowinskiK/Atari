*Raszyn 1809* is a Polish strategy game from Mirage Software (1992), written by Maciej Ślifirczyk with music by Jakub
Husak. It is about the Battle of Raszyn, fought near Warsaw on 19 April 1809 in the war between the Duchy of Warsaw
and Austria.

The game is played from the keyboard:

- After the first title screen, press any key. The game then asks *Wprowadzenie (T/N)*, whether to show the
  introduction: press **T** (yes) or **N** (no).
- At the second title screen, press **Return**. The game waits for that key only.
- Next, choose the opponent (*Przeciwnik*): the computer (*Komputer*) or another player (*Gracz*).

The game is written in Atari BASIC and packed into an executable. It switches BASIC on by itself, so the player
runs it on the built-in Altirra BASIC.
