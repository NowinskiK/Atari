*Lasermania* is a puzzle game from the Polish company LK Avalon, 1990. Mirosław Liminowicz came up with it and
programmed it with Janusz Pelc; the graphics are by Marek Siewior and Pelc, the sound by Daniel Kleczynski and Pelc.
Avalon sold it together with Pelc's *Robbo Konstruktor*.

The game's own instructions put it like this: from the XL/XE control centre you remotely steer K9, a self-propelled
tracked vehicle, through rooms where oval memory units lie scattered. Collect them, destroy the sensors of the alarm
system with the laser beam, and send headquarters the secret password you find after the last room. On the way you
meet lasers and laser bombs, timed laser switches, inner doors with their control consoles, blocks of polished steel
and direction changers. If you get stuck with no way out, press **Esc**.

Steer with the joystick or the cursor keys (here: arrows, and Space or Ctrl to fire). All the texts are in Polish.

In 2014 Heptomino and LK Avalon announced ports of *Fred* and *Lasermania* to iOS and Android, and the game was later
remade for the Atari as *Lasermania 2020*.
