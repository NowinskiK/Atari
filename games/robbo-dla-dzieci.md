*Robbo dla Dzieci* ("Robbo for Children") is a home-made *Robbo* by Aleksander Sobala of Kraków, dated 1991. Its
opening screen says this version was made with the youngest players in mind, so it should be light, easy and pleasant,
and leaves it to you to judge whether that worked. It was built with *Robbo Konstruktor* and saved as a stand-alone game.

It plays like the original: collect the screws on each planet, then reach the capsule that flies you to the next one.
Play with the joystick (here: arrows, and Space or Ctrl to fire). START (here: F2) leaves the title screen and then the
opening text. All the texts are in Polish.

Sources: the game's own texts.
