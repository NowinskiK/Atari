*Zeus* is a platform game published by LK Avalon in 1993. It was programmed by Krzysztof Szmatola, with graphics by
Tomasz Sadza and sound by Bartek Kalinowski.

You are Zeus, the god of ancient Greece, and the evil Bat has kidnapped Aphrodite. Fight your way to her through eight
levels, each of which ends with a fight against its guard. Your weapons are jumps and thunderbolts. Play with the
joystick (here: arrows, and Space or Ctrl to fire). The texts are in Polish.
