*Montezuma's Revenge* was designed and programmed by Robert Jaeger, who built the first version on an Atari 8-bit
computer under his own Utopia Software label. Parker Brothers licensed the game before it was finished and released it
in 1984 for many systems. This disk is a 1983 prototype, not that release, so it may differ from the game you remember.

You play Panama Joe in a pyramid full of traps. Walk, climb and jump from room to room, collecting keys and treasure.
Play with the joystick (here: arrows, and Space or Ctrl to jump). The game starts by itself once the disk has loaded.
