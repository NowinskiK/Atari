*Uczeń Czarnoksiężnika* ("The Sorcerer's Apprentice") is a platform adventure published by LK Avalon in 1992,
programmed by Krzysztof Szmatola with graphics by "Drops".

Your master, a great sorcerer, died while creating a spell. To take his place, find all the pages of his Magic Book,
his Magic Wand and his Magic Hat, which are hidden in a castle that is a maze of rooms. If you succeed, the whole castle
is yours and the sorcerers' guild takes you in. Play with the joystick (here: arrows, and Space or Ctrl to fire). The
texts are in Polish.
