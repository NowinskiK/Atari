*Upiór* ("The Ghoul") is a platform game published by LK Avalon in 1992. It was programmed by Rafał Wójcik, with
graphics by Wójcik and Tomasz Sadza and sound by Przemysław Witkowski. It is best remembered for its large animated
sprites.

The ghoul and his monstrous friends have taken over your castle. Search its rooms for 20 gems: you can enter the
ghoul's chamber only once you have all of them. Punch and kick the monsters, pick up chalices to heal, and do not fall
from high up, because that hurts too. You have only one life: when your health runs out, the game is over. Play with
the joystick (here: arrows, and Space or Ctrl to fire).

Not to be confused with the 2018 *Upiór* by Retronics.
